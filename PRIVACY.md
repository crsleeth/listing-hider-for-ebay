# Privacy Policy: Listing Hider for eBay

*Last updated: October 29, 2026*

Listing Hider for eBay does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

To do its job, the extension saves the following **only in your own browser** (`storage.local`):

- The IDs and titles of listings you choose to hide
- Seller names you choose to block
- Keywords you enter
- Whether the "show hidden listings" option is on

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer, to eBay, or to anyone else. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What the extension reads

On eBay pages, the extension reads the search results shown to you (listing titles, IDs and seller names) so it can decide which ones to hide. Nothing it reads is recorded beyond the items listed above or sent anywhere.

## Network requests, analytics and tracking

None. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, ads or tracking.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves your hidden listings, blocked sellers and keywords in your browser.
- **Access to eBay sites**: adds the Hide and Block seller buttons to eBay search results and hides matching listings.
